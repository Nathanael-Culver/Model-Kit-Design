# USS Defiant — Assembly Sequence

**Document status:** DRAFT PROCESS

1. Complete textual electrical design and validation.
2. Bench-test all modules individually.
3. Paint/light-block the interior surfaces that will become inaccessible.
4. Dry-fit battery, receiver coil, XIAO, boost module, NFC antenna/module, LED emitters and harness routes.
5. Build/test the complete bench harness outside the hull.
6. Transfer the approved harness into the hull while it remains reopenable.
7. Verify wireless power, deep sleep/wake, NFC, all lighting, phasers, Wi-Fi/BLE control and OTA in the installed position.
8. Record photos, component locations and routed wire lengths.
9. Secure/insulate electronics and provide strain relief.
10. Run the full pre-close test plan again.
11. Close the hull only after acceptance.
12. Perform exterior seam work, primer, painting, decals/weathering and final finish.
13. Run final functional acceptance after exterior finishing.

## Assembly constraints

- Do not use final wire lengths from estimates; derive them from the approved physical route.
- Avoid conductive foil/copper placement near the NFC antenna or wireless-power coil until RF/inductive testing proves it acceptable.
- Battery must be protected from sharp plastic edges, solder joints and compression.
- No adhesive choice becomes permanent until material compatibility and serviceability are considered.
- Every installed module/wire should be photographed before the hull is sealed.
